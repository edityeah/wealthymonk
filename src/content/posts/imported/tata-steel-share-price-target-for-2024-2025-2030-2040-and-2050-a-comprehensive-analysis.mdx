## **Tata Steel Overview**

Tata Steel, founded in 1907, is a leading global steel producer headquartered in Mumbai, India. The company operates in over 26 countries and has a significant presence in India and abroad, including listings on the **National Stock Exchange (NSE)**, **Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE)**, and the **London Stock Exchange (LSE)**. Tata Steel's impressive market position, strong financial performance, and dedication to sustainability have made it a preferred choice for investors worldwide.

The company’s extensive product portfolio caters to industries such as automotive, construction, engineering, and infrastructure. With a focus on sustainability, Tata Steel is committed to reducing its carbon footprint and promoting responsible business practices, enhancing its appeal as an investment option.
