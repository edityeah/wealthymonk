The **National Stock Exchange (NSE)**, India’s leading stock exchange, ranks as the **10th largest stock exchange globally**, with a market capitalisation exceeding **$3.4 trillion**. Known for its efficient trading operations, the NSE offers trading in equities, derivatives, and more from **9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.** on weekdays, providing investors with a trading window of **6 hours and 15 minutes**.

In addition to weekends, the NSE observes specific national and cultural holidays. On these days, **no trading** occurs across the equity, equity derivatives, and **SLB segments**. Below is a complete list of **NSE trading holidays** for 2024, along with additional details on **commodity trading holidays** and **Muhurat Trading**.

### **What is Muhurat Trading?**

**Muhurat Trading** is a special, auspicious trading session conducted on **Diwali**, one of India’s most celebrated festivals. This tradition is considered to bring prosperity and good luck. In 2024, Muhurat Trading will be held on **Friday, November 01**, with specific timings to be announced closer to the date.

Muhurat Trading is typically a one-hour session in the evening, allowing traders and investors to participate in the market during Diwali festivities. It’s a significant event for investors, symbolizing a fresh start in the financial year.
