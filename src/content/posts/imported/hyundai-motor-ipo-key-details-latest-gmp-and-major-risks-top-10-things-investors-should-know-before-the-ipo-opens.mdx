The **Hyundai Motor India IPO** is set to be one of the most significant events in India’s capital markets this year, potentially surpassing the scale of the **LIC IPO** in 2022. The public issue, opening for subscription from **October 15 to October 17, 2024**, will see shares of the Indian subsidiary of **Hyundai Motor Company** listed for the first time on Indian exchanges. The listing of **Hyundai Motor India Ltd** on the **BSE** and **NSE** marks Hyundai's first international stock market debut outside of South Korea. With a price band between **₹1,865 to ₹1,960 per share**, the IPO is expected to draw considerable interest from institutional and retail investors alike.

Here’s an in-depth look at the 10 most important things you need to know before investing in this IPO, along with key risks and the latest **grey market premium (GMP)** updates.

### **Overview of Hyundai Motor India IPO**

Hyundai Motor India’s IPO, set to launch on **October 15, 2024**, is already making headlines due to its expected scale and impact on the Indian stock market. The IPO will remain open for bidding until **October 17, 2024**, and its shares are expected to hit **D-Street** on **October 22, 2024**.

As the **second-largest car manufacturer in India** after **Maruti Suzuki**, Hyundai Motor India has been a dominant player in the country’s auto sector. It has a long history of operations, having been in the Indian market since **1996**, and offers a range of passenger vehicles, including sedans, SUVs, hatchbacks, and electric vehicles (EVs).

This IPO is set to become the largest in India, surpassing the ₹21,000 crore **LIC IPO** from May 2022. Moreover, it is one of the **biggest IPOs in Asia** in recent times, reflecting the company’s robust position in the automotive industry.

---

### **Hyundai Motor India Company Details**

**Hyundai Motor India Ltd**, a wholly-owned subsidiary of **Hyundai Motor Company** (South Korea), is a key player in the Indian automobile industry. Hyundai, along with **Kia**, forms part of the **Hyundai Motor Group**, the **third-largest auto original equipment manufacturer (OEM)** in the world, with total sales of **7.3 million vehicles** in 2023.

#### **Operations and Product Portfolio**

Hyundai Motor India focuses on the **manufacturing** and **selling** of passenger vehicles and automotive components like **transmissions** and **engines**. The company’s current vehicle portfolio features **13 models**, including:

- **Sedans**
- **Hatchbacks**
- **SUVs**
- **Battery Electric Vehicles (EVs)**

Hyundai operates a vast network of **1,366 sales points** and **1,550 service centers** across India, making it one of the most accessible brands for Indian consumers. Since its inception in India, Hyundai has sold **nearly 12 million vehicles**, with a significant portion of its output going towards exports.

#### **Market Position and Competitiveness**

According to a **CRISIL report**, Hyundai Motor India has consistently ranked as the **second-largest OEM** in the Indian passenger vehicle market in fiscal years **2022, 2023, and 2024** (up to June 30, 2024). Hyundai has maintained this position since **2009**, and its dominance continues in the face of competition from local and foreign automakers.

While other global players like **Ford** and **General Motors** have struggled to penetrate the Indian market, Hyundai has managed to establish itself as a trusted and reliable brand among Indian consumers.

### **10 Things Investors Must Know Before Subscribing to the Hyundai Motor India IPO**

#### 1. **Key Dates of Hyundai Motor India IPO**

The IPO opens for subscription on **Tuesday, October 15, 2024**, and closes on **Thursday, October 17, 2024**. The IPO’s allocation to **anchor investors** will take place on **Monday, October 14, 2024**.

Key dates:

- **Basis of Allotment Finalization**: **Friday, October 18, 2024**
- **Refunds Initiated**: **Monday, October 21, 2024**
- **Shares Credited to Demat Accounts**: **October 21, 2024**
- **Listing on BSE & NSE**: **Tuesday, October 22, 2024**

#### 2. **IPO Price Band**

The price band for Hyundai Motor India’s IPO is set at **₹1,865 to ₹1,960 per equity share**, with a face value of ₹10 per share. The **floor price** is **186.5 times** the face value, while the **cap price** is **196 times** the face value.

The cap price is expected to be **120% of the floor price**. The wide range offers some flexibility for both institutional and retail investors to participate.

#### 3. **IPO Lot Size**

The **minimum lot size** is set at **7 shares** per application, which means investors can bid for a minimum of **7 shares** or multiples thereof. The minimum investment for retail investors is approximately **₹13,720**, based on the lowest price in the band.

#### 4. **IPO Issue Details**

Hyundai Motor India is looking to raise **₹27,870 crore** (approximately **$3.3 billion**) through this IPO, which is an **Offer for Sale (OFS)**. The IPO will not involve the issuance of new shares but will instead see Hyundai Motor Company, the South Korean parent, sell **14.22 crore shares** or **17.5%** of its stake in the Indian unit.

Even after the IPO, Hyundai Motor Company will retain **82.5% ownership** of its Indian subsidiary. If shares are priced at the **upper range** of the price band, the company will be valued at **₹1,59,540 crore** ($19 billion), making it the biggest stock offering in India this year.

#### 5. **IPO Reservation for Investors**

- **50%** of the IPO will be reserved for **Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs)**.
- **15%** is allocated to **Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs)**.
- **35%** is earmarked for **Retail Investors**.
- **7,78,400 equity shares** will be reserved for the company’s **employees**.

#### 6. **Use of IPO Proceeds**

Since the IPO is entirely an **Offer for Sale (OFS)**, **Hyundai Motor India** will not receive any proceeds from the offering. The **promoter-selling shareholder** (Hyundai Motor Company) will receive the funds after deducting the IPO-related expenses and taxes.

#### 7. **Lead Managers and Registrar**

The **lead managers** (BLRMs) for the Hyundai Motor India IPO are:

- **Kotak Mahindra Capital Company Limited**
- **Citigroup Global Markets India Private Limited**
- **HSBC Securities & Capital Markets Pvt Ltd**
- **JP Morgan India Private Limited**
- **Morgan Stanley India Company Pvt Ltd**

The **registrar** for the issue is **KFin Technologies Limited**, which will handle share allocation, refunds, and other IPO-related activities.

#### 8. **Key Risks Associated with the IPO**

There are several risks that potential investors should consider before subscribing:

- **Supplier Dependency**: Hyundai Motor India depends heavily on a few suppliers for key parts and materials. Any increase in **component costs** or **supply chain disruptions** could adversely affect its operations.
- **Price Fluctuations**: Hyundai is vulnerable to **price volatility** in materials, including parts required for manufacturing **electric vehicles (EVs)** and other automotive components.
- **Market Competition**: Hyundai Motor India faces stiff competition from industry giants like **Maruti Suzuki**, **Tata Motors**, and **Mahindra & Mahindra** in the Indian passenger vehicle market.
- **Reliance on Parent Company**: The Indian subsidiary depends heavily on the **parent company** for technology, operations, and materials. Any disruption in the parent company could affect the Indian unit's performance.

#### 9. **Listed Peers**

Hyundai Motor India’s key competitors in the Indian market, as per the **Red Herring Prospectus (RHP)**, include:

- **Maruti Suzuki India**: P/E Ratio – 17.93 (as of October 4, 2024)
- **Tata Motors**: P/E Ratio – 11.36
- **Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M)**: P/E Ratio – 29.96

#### 10. **Latest Grey Market Premium (GMP)**

As of today, Hyundai Motor India’s **grey market premium (GMP)** stands at **₹60**. This suggests that the shares are trading at a premium of ₹60 over the upper price band of ₹1,960, implying a potential listing price of around **₹2,020** per share—**3.06% higher** than the IPO’s cap price.

However, the GMP has shown a **downward trend** over the last 15 trading sessions, ranging from ₹0 to ₹570. While the GMP provides insight into market sentiment, investors should exercise caution and not rely solely on this metric when making their investment decisions.
