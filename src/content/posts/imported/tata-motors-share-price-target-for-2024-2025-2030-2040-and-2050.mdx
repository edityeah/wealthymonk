Tata Motors Limited, a global automotive giant headquartered in Mumbai, has evolved significantly since its founding in 1945 as a locomotive manufacturer. By venturing into the commercial vehicle market in 1954, Tata Motors began its journey to becoming a dominant force in the automotive industry. This article aims to provide insights into Tata Motors’ share price targets for 2024, 2025, 2030, 2040, and 2050.

Over the decades, Tata Motors has expanded its portfolio with iconic models like the Tata Sierra, Tata Estate, Tata Sumo, and Tata Indica, catering to a wide range of consumer preferences. In the 2000s, the company’s aggressive growth strategy included key acquisitions like Daewoo, Marcopolo, and Jaguar Land Rover, further solidifying its global presence.

With a well-distributed network of manufacturing plants across India, including locations in Jamshedpur, Pune, Pantnagar, Sanand, Dharwad, and Lucknow, Tata Motors ensures efficient production to meet market demands. Internationally, the company has manufacturing facilities in Argentina, South Africa, Thailand, and the UK, further boosting its global footprint.

## **Tata Motors Stock Performance**

Tata Motors has consistently performed well on the **National Stock Exchange (NSE)**, with a current share price of ₹811 and a solid **dividend yield of 2.0% since 2013**. This stability, coupled with strong investor confidence, has made Tata Motors an attractive option in the automotive sector.
