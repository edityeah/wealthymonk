Over the years, Wipro has diversified its business into various sectors, including **IT services, consulting, healthcare, personal care, furniture**, and more. Headquartered in **Sarjapur, Bangalore**, Wipro is a major player in India's corporate landscape. This article provides a detailed analysis of **Wipro's share price history**, **bonus and stock split history**, and the astounding potential returns on a ₹10,000 investment made in 1980.
